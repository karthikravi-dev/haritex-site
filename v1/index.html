Certified quality, verified standards

Our GOTS, GRS and OCS certified manufacturing is independently audited by IDFL. Better Cotton is sourced for buyers whose programmes require it.

Certified

GOTS Global Organic Textile Standard

Hari Tex holds GOTS certification — the leading certification for organic textiles worldwide. GOTS requires independent, on-site auditing of the entire production chain, from raw organic fibre through to finished, labelled fabric, along with strict environmental and toxicological limits on the chemicals used in processing.

Our certification covers both dyed and greige woven organic cotton fabrics.

Certified

GRS Global Recycled Standard

We see recycled material not as a compromise but as an opportunity — giving existing textile waste a second life instead of drawing further on virgin resources. The Global Recycled Standard is the internationally recognised framework for this: a voluntary, third-party certification that verifies recycled content, full chain-of-custody traceability, and the social, environmental and chemical-safety practices behind it.

Every claim we make about recycled content is independently verified, not just stated.

Certified

OCS Organic Content Standard

Hari Tex holds OCS certification, confirming that our certified fabrics are verified for organic fibre content and tracked from source material through to finished product.

An independently verified chain of custody for organic content claims from 5% to 100%.

Sourced on request

BCI Better Cotton Initiative

One of the world’s largest cotton sustainability programmes, built to address two realities of conventional cotton farming: its heavy resource footprint, and the documented underpayment of farm workers across parts of the industry. BCI promotes more responsible water and land use, and fair pay for farmers.

We source BCI cotton for buyers as part of their sustainability requirements.
